How It Works

1

Submit Issue

Students submit complaints related to classroom, hostel, lab, cafeteria, library, or security.

2

Review & Assign

Admin reviews the issue and assigns it to the concerned maintenance staff member.

3

Resolve

Maintenance staff resolves the issue and marks it complete with a resolution note.

4

Close & Notify

Status is updated and students can track the resolution of their complaint.

Key Features

📋

Category-wise Tracking

Complaints are organized by category — classroom, hostel, lab, cafeteria, library, security.

🔥

Priority-based Handling

Issues are tagged High, Medium, or Low priority so urgent problems are addressed first.

📊

Status Tracking

Real-time status updates: Submitted → In Progress → Resolved.

🛡️

Admin Dashboard

Admins get a full overview of all complaints with assignment and management tools.

⏱️

Resolution Monitoring

Track how long each complaint takes from submission to resolution.

🔧

Staff Portal

Maintenance staff have their own portal to view and resolve assigned issues.

User Roles

🎓

Student

Submit complaints, track their resolution status, and stay informed about updates to their reported issues.

🛡️

Admin

Review all complaints, assign them to staff, monitor overall performance, and close resolved issues.

🔧

Maintenance Staff

View complaints assigned to them, take action, and update status with resolution notes.
